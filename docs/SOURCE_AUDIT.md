# Attached-source audit

## Iran Defence Products, Issue 2013-2016

The supplied PDF is a 346-page Mindex Center commercial compilation titled *Iran Defence Products*. A copy is retained at `docs/sources/iran_defence_products_2013_2016.pdf` for auditability. It is not a missile-threat assessment and is not evidence that Hezbollah possesses every listed product.

The v0.7 review used these catalog sections:

| PDF page | Product | Safe data treatment |
|---:|---|---|
| 154 | Basic Toophan | Advertised SACLOS architecture and 0.07-3.85 km product envelope support the broad family record; advertised reliability and penetration are excluded |
| 155 | Toophan-2 | Distinct tandem-warhead product retained as a product-only research candidate |
| 156 | Toophan-3 | Distinct 0.65-3.5 km top-attack/EFP product retained as a product-only research candidate |
| 158 | Saeghe | Distinct light anti-armor product retained as a product-only research candidate |
| 159 | Saeghe-2 | Distinct tandem-warhead light product retained as a product-only research candidate |
| 160 | Fadjr-1 107 mm | Useful product context; already covered at historical family level by the selected CSIS source |

The catalog did not yield variant-level entries for Fadi, Malyutka/Sagger, Fagot, Konkurs, Metis, Kornet, Dehlavieh, or Towsan in the reviewed text. Its many gun and small-arms pages are not imported as Hezbollah inventory because a product catalog cannot establish faction attribution.

No armor-penetration figures, operating procedures, current quantities, deployment locations, or target-specific effects are imported.
