# OSINT and manual-data policy

The OSINT baseline contains cited public facts plus explicitly labeled category-level engineering assessments. Unsupported factual fields remain `null` and are never silently estimated.

Manual mode applies a separate JSON overlay in memory. It can change quantities, model indices, readiness assumptions, APS coverage, endurance, or ceiling values without rewriting the baseline.

Quantity scopes such as contracted procurement, program ceiling, delivered inventory, estimated inventory, and available inventory are not interchangeable.

The project excludes current unit locations, special-forces locations, available-now force status, base vulnerabilities, active patrol plans, strategic-facility locations, supply-route geolocation, and target-specific strike effects.

## Weapon evidence

- Historical possession or use never becomes a current stock count.
- A family-level range is not silently assigned to every variant.
- CEP is stored only when a source actually reports CEP. Impact-area estimates and operator-dependent direct-fire systems use different accuracy measures.
- Weather fields are qualitative source-backed or engineering-category assessments. Numeric weather performance is unavailable in realistic mode until a weapon-specific empirical curve is sourced.
- A social-media post may be recorded as a lead, but it does not become baseline evidence without corroboration and an explicit caveat.
- A manufacturer or commercial catalog proves only that a product was advertised. It does not prove Hezbollah possession, current availability, field reliability, or combat performance.
- User-supplied designations remain in the research queue when the exact name or variant cannot be corroborated.
- No launch locations, current firing units, stockpile sites, target sets, penetration planning, or operational recommendations are stored.

## Village-map layer

- v0.8 village maps are fictional, deterministic training grids and are not georeferenced.
- Sector/village placeholder names do not encode real facilities, positions, patrol areas, or target locations.
- v1.1 adds a separate public-geography theater layer with WGS84 borders, roads, water, contours and settlement labels. It is not a current operational picture and is not used to place units or facilities.
- Public city/village coordinates may identify ordinary settlements; no current military deployments, target sets, strategic-weapon facilities, inferred hidden sites, active routes, or faction-linked civilian locations are stored.
- Elevation contours remain visual until a later validated raster sampler explicitly binds DEM values to tactical physics.
- The 13×13 Alpha–Mike / 01–13 / keypad system is a simulation coordinate scheme, not a real-world operational grid.

## v0.9 spatial and vehicle boundary

- Local X/Y/Z values are simulator coordinates in meters, not real-world geodetic coordinates.
- Ground Z for village scenarios is deterministic synthetic terrain and is not imported from real DEM/elevation services.
- LOS calculations operate only on this local synthetic world in the shipped scenarios.
- Faction detection uses normalized synthetic sensor/signature values; the radar channel is not real RCS.
- Modern armor uses synthetic 0–1000 protection indexes and does not claim classified armor thickness/RHAe.
- APS game countermeasure budgets and sensor-quality values are simulation parameters, not real inventories/performance.
- The IDF organization dataset is a public peacetime reference tree; it is not a current deployment/order-of-battle feed.
- Public vehicle dimensions/crew/speed/engine fields are separated from game-only performance parameters.

## v1.7 continuous-world and equipment boundary

- Continuous local XYZ positions are synthetic scenario coordinates. They must not be treated as current real-world tactical coordinates.
- Formation centroids/dispersion are derived from those synthetic member positions; they are not externally sourced deployments.
- `v1_7_integrated_scenario.json` lower-level ORBAT assignments and weapon allocations are synthetic integration-test content.
- `hezbollah_small_arms_osint_v1_0.json` is an evidence/provenance catalog of publicly identified weapon presence. It does not establish quantities, prevalence, standard issue, current unit allocation, storage location, serviceability, or current deployment.
- Runtime reliability, accuracy, suppression, range and other combat coefficients generated by `SmallArmsRuntimeAdapter` are normalized simulation values unless explicitly marked otherwise.
- Cartridge-family data in v1.7 is identity-only. No real projectile penetration, wound, barrier or armor performance table is included.
- Structure/CQB models introduced in v1.7 are generic synthetic data contracts and do not contain real bunker/hideout locations or construction plans.


## v1.9 support-data boundary
Named public platforms may be used as labels, but support zones, quantities, readiness, cue uncertainty, mission timings, fuel/store states and all placements are synthetic. Counter-battery/air-support behavior is a game abstraction and must not be treated as real targeting guidance.

## v1.9.2 country-scale support placement policy

The country-scale web sandbox may display real place names as coarse scenario labels, but the simulation coordinates attached to artillery, radar, rocket, UAV and fixed-wing assets are synthetic offsets. They are not exact current deployment coordinates or firing positions. The counter-battery cue and impact system uses deliberately abstract uncertainty/effects logic and must not be treated as real targeting guidance.
